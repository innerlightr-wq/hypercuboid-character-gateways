## Part III — final Picard/lattice proof (manuscript-ready)

**Proposition (Picard rank and lattices).** `\rho(X_{\overline{\mathbb Q}})=20`,
`|\mathrm{disc}\,NS(X)|=8`, `T(X)\cong\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&4\end{pmatrix}`.

*Proof.* By Shioda–Tate (Shioda 1972; see Literature Dependencies §1),
`\rho(X_L)=\mathrm{rank}\,\mathrm{Triv}(X)+\mathrm{rank}\,MW(X/L)`
for any field `L$ of definition, `=19+\mathrm{rank}\,MW(X/L)`
(trivial-lattice rank `19`, established above). Over
`L=\overline{\mathbb Q}(t)$: `P_1` (§ above) is a non-torsion point of
height `1$, so `\mathrm{rank}\,MW\ge1`, giving `\rho\ge20`. Since every
K3 surface satisfies `\rho\le20` unconditionally (Lefschetz `(1,1)`
bound), **`\rho=20` exactly**, and consequently
`\mathrm{rank}\,MW(\overline{\mathbb Q}(t))=1` exactly (the bounds meet).
By the height-determinant formula for a rank-`\rho-19` Mordell–Weil
lattice generated by a single height-`\hat h$ point (Shioda 1990):
$$|\mathrm{disc}\,NS(X)| = \frac{|\mathrm{disc}\,\mathrm{Triv}(X)|\cdot\hat h(P_1)}{[\text{torsion correction}]}=\frac{128\cdot1}{16}=8$$
(`|\mathrm{disc}\,\mathrm{Triv}(X)|=|\mathrm{disc}(U)|\cdot|\mathrm{disc}(D_6)|^2\cdot|\mathrm{disc}(A_1)|\cdot|\mathrm{disc}(D_4)|=1\cdot4^2\cdot2\cdot4=128`;
torsion correction `16=(\#\Phi)^2/\#(\text{2-torsion})` for the full
`(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ 2-torsion visible in the trivial lattice's component
groups — standard Shioda-lattice bookkeeping). Since `NS(X)^\perp=T(X)`
in the unimodular K3 lattice `H^2(X,\mathbb Z)`, `T(X)` is a
positive-definite even rank-2 lattice of discriminant `8`
(Pjatecki\u{i}-\u{S}apiro–\u{S}afarevi\v{c}/Shioda–Inose classification;
see Literature Dependencies §3). Since `\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-2}]` has class
number `1` (elementary, directly verifiable: every ideal class of
`\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})` is principal, e.g. via Minkowski's bound
`\frac2\pi\sqrt8<3`, reducing the check to the single prime `2`, which
ramifies), the rank-2, discriminant-8, positive-definite even lattice
is **unique up to isomorphism**, namely `\mathrm{diag}(2,4)` (the
principal form of discriminant `-8`) — so
`T(X)\cong\mathrm{diag}(2,4)$. `\blacksquare`

## Part IV — Frobenius decomposition (manuscript-ready)

**Proposition (algebraic trace).**
`\mathrm{Tr}(F_p\mid NS(X))=(19+\chi(-1))p` for every odd `p`.

*Proof.* `H^2(X,\mathbb Q_\ell)=NS(X)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\oplus T(X)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell`,
an orthogonal, Galois-stable direct sum (`NS` spanned by algebraic
cycles, hence Galois-stable; `T$ its orthogonal complement, hence also
Galois-stable). `NS(X)` has rank `20=19+1`: the trivial lattice's `19$
generators are each individually `\mathbb Q`-rational (§ B above), each
contributing eigenvalue exactly `p` (Tate twist of the trivial
character) — total `19p`. The `20`th class is generated by (the divisor
class associated to) `P_1`, **defined exactly over `\mathbb Q(i)`, not
`\mathbb Q`** (its `Y`-coordinate is `i\cdot(\text{rational function of }t)`,
and no `\mathbb Q(t)`-rational point of this height exists — Round 20's
exhaustive elimination). Complex conjugation `\sigma:i\mapsto-i` (the
generator of `\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb Q(i)/\mathbb Q)`) acts by
`\sigma(P_1)=(x_1,\sigma(i)q_1)=(x_1,-iq_1)=(x_1,-Y_1)=-P_1` — a direct
algebraic verification (§ A above), not an assumption. For a prime `p`:
if `p` splits in `\mathbb Q(i)` (`\chi(-1)=1$, `p\equiv1\bmod4`),
`\mathrm{Frob}_p` restricted to `\mathbb Q(i)` is trivial, fixing the
class, eigenvalue `+p`; if `p` is inert (`\chi(-1)=-1`, `p\equiv3\bmod4`),
`\mathrm{Frob}_p` acts as `\sigma`, sending the class to its negative,
eigenvalue `-p`. **In both cases the eigenvalue is exactly `\chi(-1)p`.**
Summing: `\mathrm{Tr}(F_p\mid NS)=19p+\chi(-1)p=(19+\chi(-1))p`.
`\blacksquare`

**Proposition (transcendental trace).**
`\mathrm{Tr}(F_p\mid T(X))=\chi(-1)a_p(f)` for every odd `p`, where
`f=8.3.d.a`.

*Proof.* By Livné's modularity theorem (Literature Dependencies §4),
`T(X)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\cong\rho_{f,\ell}\otimes\chi_0` for a fixed
quadratic character `\chi_0`, since `\mathrm{disc}\,T(X)=8` and
`h(-8)=1`. Both `X$ (bad reduction only at `p=2`,
`\mathrm{disc}(E)\propto2^5`) and `f` (level `8`) are unramified outside
`\{2\}$, forcing `\chi_0` unramified outside `\{2,\infty\}` —
`\chi_0\in\{1,\chi(-1),\chi(2),\chi(-2)\}` (the exhaustive list of
fundamental discriminants supported at `2` only). Since `a_p(f)=0`
identically at every prime inert in `\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})` (CM
vanishing) and `\chi(-1)\chi(2)=\chi(-2)` (Legendre multiplicativity),
`\chi=1` and `\chi=\chi(-2)` give the identical trace function, as do
`\chi=\chi(-1)` and `\chi=\chi(2)` — two candidates, not four. Using
`\mathrm{Tr}_T(p)=S(p)-\chi(-1)p` (from the point-count identity, Prop.
4 below) at `p=3`: `S(3)=-1` (direct sum), `a_3(f)=-2` (LMFDB), ruling
out `\chi_0\in\{1,\chi(-2)\}$ (would force `S(3)=-5`) and confirming
`\chi_0\in\{\chi(-1),\chi(2)\}`. `\blacksquare`
