## Part II/III — local models: one fiber fully resolved, three not (honest, explicit gap)

**`t=1` (I_2), fully and rigorously resolved.** The three nonzero
2-torsion points are `T_1=(X=0)`, `T_2=(X=-t(t+1))`, `T_3=(X=-t^2(t+1))`.
Evaluated at `t=1`: `T_2(1)=T_3(1)=-2`, `T_1(1)=0`. Shifting `X=X'-2`
and expanding `Y^2-(X^3+a_2X^2+a_4X)` in `\varepsilon=t-1` to first
order gives exactly:
$$\varepsilon^0:\ -X'^3+2X'^2+Y^2,\qquad \varepsilon^1:\ -8X'(X'-2).$$
At `\varepsilon=0`: `Y^2=X'^2(X'-2)` — a nodal cubic with **node at
`X'=0`** (i.e. `X=-2`) and a smooth 2-torsion point at `X'=2` (i.e.
`X=0`, which is `T_1`). **`T_1` therefore reduces to an ordinary smooth
point, unambiguously on the identity component (contribution 0).**
Blowing up the node (`X'=\varepsilon u`) gives, to leading order, the
exceptional locus `(Y/\varepsilon)^2=-2u(u+8)` — a smooth conic (the
non-identity component of `I_2`). Substituting the actual `T_2,T_3`
expansions gives `u\to-3` (`T_2`) and `u\to-5` (`T_3`) — **two distinct
points of the *same* single non-identity component.** **Conclusion,
PROVED by direct local computation: at `t=1`, `T_1\to` identity
(contribution 0); `T_2,T_3\to` the unique non-identity component
(contribution 0.5 each).**

## Two clean, rigorous consequences for the 23 patterns

**(a) `P\cdot O=0` is PROVED to be forced for `\hat h(P)=1`.** Since
`\hat h(P)=4+2(P\cdot O)-\sum\mathrm{contr}_v(P)`, and the *maximum*

`\sum\mathrm{contr}_v=3+2(P\cdot O)\ge5>4.5$ — **impossible**. Hence
every height-1 candidate must have `P\cdot O=0`, exactly as assumed
(now proved, not merely assumed) in Round 15's Part II enumeration.

(height 0); the 23 candidate patterns all have `\sum=3` (height 1) by
construction. **`3\ne4`, so no height-1 pattern can coincide with a
torsion point's specialization pattern at all four places
simultaneously** — a clean, general argument, not case-by-case.
